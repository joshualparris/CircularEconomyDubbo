# Council e-waste collection boundary — 20 October 2026

**Status: research, NOT authority to collect or receive Council e-waste.**

Dubbo Regional Council has declined the proposed separate stream for reusable laptops and desktops at Whylandra at this stage. Council's existing e-waste arrangement continues. A future arrangement would require its own assessment, approvals and documented agreement. Publicly note this status only; the Council correspondence is not reproduced here.

### Separation of programmes

- **DubboEwaste / AssetFlow ITAD:** prospective collection, transfer of ownership, data sanitisation, refurbishment, donation/sale, residual recycling. Requires independent business regulatory checks AND any applicable Council/contract permissions. Source-of-truth dossier: [20 Oct Council approval dossier](https://github.com/joshualparris/DubboEwaste/blob/main/docs/COUNCIL-WHYLANDRA-APPROVAL-DOSSIER-2026-10-20.md); [deep research prompt](https://github.com/joshualparris/DubboEwaste/blob/main/docs/DEEP-RESEARCH-PROMPT-COUNCIL-WHYLANDRA-2026-10-20.md).
- **Repair Café Dubbo:** visitor keeps possession of their own device at a separate, agreed event. Host permission, organiser capacity, valid insurance covering repair risk, child/electrical safety and volunteer arrangements remain independent open gates. Council interest/discussion is NOT Council operation or insurance.
- **CircularEconomyDubbo public guidance:** may describe legal direct-donor, repair, reuse and existing lawful recycling destinations, but must not suggest collection at Council sites by this project is operating or approved.

### Cross-programme assurance questions

1. Which entity signs agreements and owns equipment or carries responsibility for repairs?
2. Is donor/customer property retained onsite or taken offsite; what consent and data-security obligations arise in each case?
3. What insurer confirms the exact e-waste refurbishment and/or Repair Café activities in writing? Do not assume one policy covers both.
4. Which Council branch is making a decision: waste contractor arrangement, public-event venue, grant eligibility or public communications? Approvals do not transfer between categories.
5. What evidence can safely be shared publicly (anonymised impact statistics, external citations) versus kept private (custody manifests, real serials, donor data, incident reports and emails)?
